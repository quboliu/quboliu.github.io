Permissions are still a major pain point in today's agent systems. Across the various agents I've used, there are rigid permission restrictions on one hand, and on the other you never really know when a task is suddenly going to need your approval. If you don't receive the permission prompt immediately, the task just sits there stuck halfway—which means whenever you delegate work to an agent, there's always a thread in the back of your mind quietly draining your attention. Today, for instance, I asked a personal agent to keep an eye on a podcast, summarize new episodes, and email me the summaries. Everything went smoothly in our conversation, yet I waited forever and no email arrived. When I checked, it was stuck waiting on send permission—and when I asked, it replied, "That wasn't me asking you—it was the system's tool-permission gate." Honestly, there's not much you can do but sigh.
